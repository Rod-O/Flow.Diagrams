- `BaseLinkModel.VertexAdded`, raised by `AddVertex` and by the new `BaseLinkModel.InsertVertex(index, position)`. A vertex the user creates by pressing on a segmentable link now raises it, so vertex edits can be tracked through `VertexAdded` and `VertexRemoved` instead of comparing `Vertices`.
- `NodeModel.SetSize(size)` resizes a node the way the renderer does after measuring it: it sets `Size`, refreshes the node and its links and re-measures its ports. Use it for `ControlledSize` nodes instead of setting `Size` and calling `Refresh()` and `ReinitializePorts()` by hand. The `Size` setter is unchanged.
