- `Diagram.ZoomToFit` no longer leaves refreshes suspended forever when something throws in the middle (a `ZoomChanged` or `PanChanged` handler); it applies zoom and pan in one `Batch`. It also does nothing while the canvas has no width or height (a canvas in a hidden tab), where it used to throw `ArgumentException` from `SetZoom(0)` and leave the diagram unable to redraw. Inside an outer `Batch` it now leaves the refresh to that batch instead of ending the suspension early.
- `KeyboardShortcutsDefaults.DeleteSelection` (the Delete key) restores refreshes and refreshes the diagram when a delete constraint throws; it used to leave the diagram unable to redraw.
- Links, groups, ports and link vertices no longer render a second time on the first unrelated canvas render after they mount, and no element renders again after its model changed twice while a render was already queued.
