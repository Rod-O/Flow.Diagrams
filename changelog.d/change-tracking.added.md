- `ModelChanges` (`None`, `Appearance`, `Geometry`), a flags enum saying why a model changed, `Model.MarkChanged(ModelChanges)` to report a change, and `SetField` for subclasses that want a property to mark its model when its value changes.
- Setting a property now redraws the model and what depends on it, with no `Refresh()` call: `Locked`, `Selected`, `Position`, `Title`, `Size`, a port's `Position` and `Size`, a link's `Router`, `PathGenerator`, markers, `Segmentable`, `Color`, `SelectedColor` and `Width`, a label's `Content`, `Distance` and `Offset`, and `SetSource`, `SetTarget`, `AddLabel`, `AddVertex` and a vertex's `SetPosition`. The guides list what each member marks.
- A diagram flushes the changes once per `Diagram.Batch` and once per input event, and recomputes geometry (link routes, group bounds) once per flush, passing it on to dependents. Outside a batch or an input event a change flushes at once. Mutating `BaseLinkModel.Labels` or `Vertices` directly is not tracked.
